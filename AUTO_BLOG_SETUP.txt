BLACKSTONE DAILY BLOG AUTOMATION

The website now includes blog.html and education.html.

To make blog posts publish automatically every day, the static Cloudflare upload needs one additional backend step. Recommended architecture:
1. Cloudflare Worker with a Cron Trigger (daily).
2. Worker retrieves trusted sources (market/rate data plus official California/municipal property-management rule sources).
3. AI model creates a short original summary with source links and a clear educational/not-legal-advice notice.
4. Store posts in Cloudflare D1 or KV.
5. blog.html fetches and displays the newest posts.

Do not auto-publish legal/rule-change claims without linking to an official source.
