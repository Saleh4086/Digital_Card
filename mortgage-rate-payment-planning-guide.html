Mortgage Rates and Monthly Payments: How Buyers Can Plan More Accurately

Understand how mortgage rates, loan amount, taxes, insurance and other costs affect the monthly payment shown by a mortgage calculator.

By Sal Gharibyar, Broker/Owner · DRE #01418692

This article provides general educational information. Real estate, lending, tax and legal circumstances vary by property and person.

Rates are only one part of the payment

The principal-and-interest payment changes with the loan amount, term and interest rate. Property taxes, insurance, HOA dues and mortgage insurance can substantially increase the total monthly obligation.

A market index is not a personal quote

Published conventional and FHA rate indexes are broad benchmarks. A lender quote depends on credit, down payment, loan type, points, occupancy, property type and market conditions.

Small rate changes can affect purchasing power

A higher rate may increase the payment for the same loan amount. Buyers can respond by changing the price range, down payment, loan structure or seller-credit strategy.

Compare rate and closing costs together

A lower rate may require discount points or other upfront costs. Compare the annual percentage rate, cash to close and expected ownership period.

Use the calculator as a planning tool

The Blackstone mortgage calculator can estimate payments with the current market benchmark, but buyers should obtain a lender-specific Loan Estimate before making a financing decision.
