Meet Sal

Experience that reaches beyond the transaction.

Sal Gharibyar has worked in real estate since 2004, helping buyers, sellers, investors and rental-property owners navigate changing markets throughout the East Bay. His approach combines hands-on brokerage experience, negotiation, investment perspective, renovation knowledge and property-management coordination.

Blackstone was built around a simple idea: clients should be able to rely on one experienced relationship whether they are purchasing a first home, selling, acquiring an investment, improving a property, leasing it or eventually selling it.
